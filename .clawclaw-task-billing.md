## The product decision (fixed, do not re-decide)
An operator pays/collects from THEIR guests for the Sovereign pass. Guest picks a pass on the operator's store page → sees the OPERATOR's bank info → uploads a success-payment receipt photo + clicks "make purchase" → the OPERTOR is notified in their own Telegram with guest-username + uuid + item + time + receipt photo → operator taps "approve" or "reject" → approve GRANTS the Sovereign entitlement immediately (one tap, no second step), reject grants nothing.
